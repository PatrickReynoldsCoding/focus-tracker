Focus Tracker · phone mockups · 15 Nov 2026

To do today, slimmer, on a whole phone screen

Each screen is 360 × 800, a standard 20:9 phone, with Today as it is now: the header, the compact focus card and your pill row. The same example day goes in each: 5 habits and 3 to-dos. Nothing scrolls, so you can see what fits.

The list is its own panel, so it can be a pill. Here it is the first pill, "Today", with a count of what is left. Tap the circles and squares to try them.

ASlim checklist

One line per thing, 42 px high. Habits, then to-dos. "Not today" is a small link on weekly habits. Done ones fold into one line at the bottom.

BHabit chips

Habits as tap chips, several to a line. Dashed chips are weekly: a long press gives "Not today". To-dos stay a short list underneath. The most compact one.

CIn the focus card

No pill needed. The pinned focus card shows a progress bar and the next three things. "+5 more" opens the rest. You see it whichever pill is open.

DAll done (A, B or C)

When everything is done the list is one line and the pill shows ✓. "Show" opens what you did, to take something back.
